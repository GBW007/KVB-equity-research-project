# Karur Vysya Bank (NSE: KARURVYSYA) – Initiating Coverage

Student equity research project: a full bank operating model, a two-method valuation and a 6-page initiation report.
**Call (30-Sep-2026): HOLD, 12-month fair value Rs300 vs CMP Rs325 (-8%). Bear Rs159 / Bull Rs372 / probability-weighted Rs283.**

**Thesis in one line:** KVB's quality is real (gross NPA 0.75%, PCR 96.5%, ROE ~19%), but ~Rs679cr of FY26 profit came from recoveries on old written-off loans (~40 bp of ROA). As they fade, sustainable ROE is ~17%; the Rs325 price already assumes ~17.4%.

## How the workbook is organised
- **Cover** – key outputs, how to use, sources, disclaimer.
- **Model** – balance sheet, P&L, margins, asset quality, per-share data, capital adequacy and a DuPont ROA tree. Scenario switch in `B3` (1 Bear, 2 Base, 3 Bull); the deltas in rows 5–8 are added to the base inputs.
- **Valuation** – CAPM cost of equity, justified P/ABV, residual income, 70/30 blend, rating rule, multiples, two sensitivity grids, scenario table.
- **Quarterly** – 13 quarters (Jun-23 to Jun-26) with YoY growth. **Peers** – 20 private banks from Screener's industry table.

Colour code: blue = input, black = formula, green = link to another sheet, yellow = key assumption.

**Reverse check:** at 1.75x Sep-27E adjusted book, the price implies ROE = g + P/B × (Ke − g) = 8.5% + 1.75 × 5.1% ≈ 17.4%.
